Luki Becker (she/they) is a media artist and composer. She currently lives in Hamburg, where she studies Multimedia Composition with Alexander Schubert. Her work engages with the computer game as an interactive medium and investigates gaming as a contemporary performance practice. Among her works are installative simulations that audiences can interact with, and staged pieces activated by performers. The starting point for Luki’s work is often musical concepts and setups, as well as their political and social implications.
